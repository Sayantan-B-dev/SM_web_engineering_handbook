### User Action Tracking
Track the workflows that matter: "User started checkout", "User submitted payment", "User completed purchase". A fast page that can't complete the purchase is still broken.

// Faro captures geolocation automatically

### Session Replay
Grafana Cloud's Session Replay lets you **visually replay what a user saw and did** inside your web application.

### Error Impact Prioritization
Grafana's redesigned errors view **triage errors by user impact** rather than volume. An error affecting 10,000 users is more important than one affecting 100.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **No user action tracking** → you know the app is fast but not whether users are succeeding.
- **No session replay** → debugging a user-reported issue is guesswork.
- **Error prioritization by volume** → you fix the most frequent error, not the most impactful one.
- **No synthetic monitoring** → you find out your site is down from Twitter, not your alerts.
- **No SLOs** → you don't know when to roll back vs when to tolerate degraded performance.
