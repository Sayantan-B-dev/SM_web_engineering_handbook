### What AI-Assisted Decision Making Is
LLM-driven human-AI collaborative decision support systems are emerging for complex industrial processes. With DeepSeek-V3 as the driving LLM, one system achieves **99.1% answer correctness** in industrial preheating tests under practical inference-time constraints.

### Hybrid Neuro-Symbolic Frameworks
TwinCompass provides both **manual expert-driven and automated LLM-based judgment mechanisms**, enabling scalable, adaptive, and context-aware recommendation generation. It integrates expert knowledge with computational intelligence to support informed, advisable engineering choices.

### The AI Referee for Technical Trade-offs
A deterministic vs LLM-based decision referee for technical trade-offs formalizes trade-offs where **every score is tied to a dimension** (performance, learning curve, scalability, flexibility) and supports dual execution modes: local deterministic logic or cloud-based AI reasoning.

### What AI Cannot Do
The strategic decisions - where to render, how to cache, when to hydrate - still require **human judgment**. AI is making us more productive at the execution layer, but strategic decisions need human context. The most successful React teams in 2026 stay pragmatic: adopting new patterns when they solve real problems, choosing stable tooling, and using AI to accelerate delivery without losing sight of fundamentals.

### Deeper Explanation: Models Accelerate Analysis, Not Judgement

AI is genuinely useful in the research and articulation parts of a decision: summarising options, drafting the ADR, generating the risk list, exploring counter-arguments, and producing the matrix skeleton. It is unreliable exactly where decisions get hard: knowing your organisation's constraints, judging operational maturity, and weighing consequences that do not appear in the text.

The productive division: use the model to expand and stress-test your thinking (ask for the strongest counter-argument to your recommendation, list failure modes you missed), then make and own the decision yourself. Never let the model's confidence substitute for your team's context.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Model output as the analysis of record.** Plausible text without your constraints is a liability.
- **Confirmation bias at scale.** Asking a model to justify a choice produces confident justification.
- **Data leakage in prompts.** Architecture docs and vendor contracts contain sensitive information.
- **No human owner.** Accountability cannot be delegated to a probabilistic system.
- **Skipping the honest counter-argument step.** Asking "what would make this wrong" is the highest-value use.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **AI as the sole decision-maker** → AI lacks context about team skills, business constraints, and organizational dynamics.
- **No evidence tagging** → AI scores can be hallucinated. Always verify.
- **Skipping human judgment** → strategic decisions require human context that AI doesn't have.
