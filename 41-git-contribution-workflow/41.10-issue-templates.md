### The Mental Model
An issue template is **input validation for humans**: it makes the reporter supply the information a maintainer needs before anyone spends time on the issue. The measurable goal is fewer round trips â€” a good template turns "it doesn't work" into a reproducible report on the first try.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **Templates with no required fields.** People submit the shortest possible text; nothing is reproducible.
- **No security contact link.** Vulnerability reports arrive as public issues and become an incident.
- **One template for everything.** Feature requests and bugs need different prompts to be useful.
- **Templates that ask for data nobody reads.** Every field should change a maintainer decision.
- **Blank issues enabled alongside templates.** Most reports arrive blank; disable it or accept the noise.
- **No auto-labels.** Triage starts from zero on every issue; labels make queues visible.
