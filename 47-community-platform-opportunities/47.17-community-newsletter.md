## 47.17 Community Newsletter

### Subtopic Tree
- Definition: periodic email digest of community updates
- Newsletter sections (upcoming events, recent talks, new projects, job listings, contributor spotlight)
- Frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
- Subscription management (opt-in, unsubscribe)
- Personalization (based on skills and interests)
- Integration with all platform features
- The "engagement" problem: email vs WhatsApp vs LinkedIn

### What a Community Newsletter Is
A community newsletter is a **periodic email digest** that keeps members engaged between events. It answers: **"What happened in the community this month?"**

The newsletter is the **engagement layer**. Events happen monthly. The newsletter fills the gap.

### Newsletter Sections
```text
React Kolkata Newsletter - October 2026
─────────────────────────────────────────
📅 Upcoming Events
  - November Online Meetup (Nov 15)
  - December Offline Meetup (Dec 20)

🎤 Recent Talks
  - "React Query Is All You Need" (recording)
  - "Observability for React in Production" (slides)

🚀 New Projects
  - 5 new projects submitted to Project Parbon

💼 Job Opportunities
  - 3 new React roles in Kolkata

🏆 Contributor Spotlight
  - This month's top contributor: [Name]

📚 Learning Resources
  - New learning path: React Server Components
```

### Frequency
- **Weekly** - too frequent. Members unsubscribe.
- **Bi-weekly** - the sweet spot for active communities.
- **Monthly** - too infrequent. Engagement drops.

**React Kolkata's recommended frequency: bi-weekly.**

### Personalization
The newsletter can be personalized based on a member's **skills and interests**:
- React Query talks for members interested in state management
- Next.js talks for members interested in frameworks
- Job listings matching their skills

---

### Deeper Explanation: A Newsletter Is A Rhythm Device

The community newsletter creates the rhythm that keeps a community alive between events: what happened, what is coming, who did something notable, and what to read. Its value is consistency and usefulness per issue â€” a monthly issue people read beats a sporadic one they ignore.

Practical design: a fixed section structure (events, call for speakers, jobs, highlights, learning), predictable cadence, and a plain, mobile-friendly template. Deliver with a real provider (deliverability, unsubscribe, and tracking), keep a web archive for SEO and sharing, and measure opens/clicks per section so you can drop what nobody reads.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Irregular cadence.** People forget they subscribed; engagement collapses.
- **No unsubscribe.** It is a legal and trust requirement.
- **Republishing whole articles.** Summarise and link; the email is a router, not the destination.
- **Sending from an unverified domain.** Deliverability and spoofing risk; configure SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
- **No archive.** New members cannot see what they missed.
- **No measurement.** Without per-section metrics you cannot improve or prune.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **No newsletter** → members engage once a month at meetups and disappear in between.
- **Weekly newsletter** → members unsubscribe. It's too much.
- **No personalization** → the newsletter is generic. Members skim and delete.
- **No unsubscribe** → members mark it as spam. Deliverability drops.
