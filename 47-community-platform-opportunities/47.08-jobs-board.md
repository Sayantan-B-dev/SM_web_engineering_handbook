## 47.8 Jobs Board

### Subtopic Tree
- Definition: curated job listings relevant to the community
- Job sources (community members, partner companies, sponsors)
- Job metadata (title, company, location, remote, skills, salary range)
- Applicant tracking (who applied, status)
- Referral mechanism (community members refer candidates)
- Company profiles (partner companies)
- Job alerts (notify members of matching jobs)

### What a Jobs Board Is
A jobs board is a **curated listing of job opportunities relevant to React Kolkata members**. It answers: **"Who is hiring React developers in Kolkata?"**

The jobs board is a **value exchange**: companies get access to a curated talent pool, and community members get access to opportunities. The community is the intermediary.

### Job Board Data Model
```text
Job:
─────────────────────────────────
id: uuid
title: string ("Senior Frontend Engineer (React)")
company: Company
location: string (Kolkata, Remote, Hybrid)
remote: boolean
skills: string[] (React, TypeScript, GraphQL)
experience_level: enum (junior, mid, senior, lead)
salary_range: string (₹18L-₹25L)
description: text
posted_at: timestamp
posted_by: Member | Company
status: enum (active, closed, filled)
referral_available: boolean
```

### Job Sources
- **Community members** - "My company is hiring"
- **Partner companies** - DevPath, Unstop, and other community partners
- **Sponsors** - Companies that sponsor React Kolkata events
- **Direct submissions** - Companies that reach out to the community

### Referral Mechanism
Community members can mark themselves as **available for referrals**. When a job matches their network, they can refer a candidate. This creates a **trust layer** between companies and candidates.

---

### Deeper Explanation: Trust And Transparency Are The Product

A jobs board in a community lives on trust: if it becomes a spam channel or hides pay and location, members stop looking. The features that preserve trust are transparency (salary range, remote policy, tech stack, interview process) and moderation (verified companies, reports, expiry on stale posts).

For candidates the value is context: roles from companies whose engineers are in the community, with an explicit "who you will work with" link. For companies the value is targeting: a small, engaged audience beats a giant one with no relevance.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **No salary transparency.** It attracts low-quality postings and erodes trust.
- **Unverified companies.** Spam and scams damage the community's credibility.
- **Posts that never expire.** The board fills with dead roles.
- **No reporting path.** Members cannot flag a bad actor.
- **Charging members to see roles.** It monetises the wrong side; charge the employers.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **No jobs board** → job opportunities are shared in WhatsApp and lost within hours.
- **No curation** → the board fills with spam and low-quality listings.
- **No referral mechanism** → the community has no way to vouch for candidates.
- **No company profiles** → members don't know which companies are community-friendly.
