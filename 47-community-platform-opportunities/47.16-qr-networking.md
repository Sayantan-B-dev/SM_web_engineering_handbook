## 47.16 QR Networking

### What QR Networking Is
QR networking is a **frictionless way to exchange profiles at events**. It answers: **"How do I connect with this person I just met?"**

The problem: exchanging LinkedIn profiles at events is awkward. You spell your name, they search, they can't find you, you spell it again. QR networking replaces this with a **single scan**.

### How It Works
```text
1. Member opens their React Kolkata profile
2. QR code is displayed (unique to them)
3. Other member scans the QR code
4. Profile opens → "Connect" button
5. Connection request sent (mutual opt-in)
6. Both members are now connected
```

### Offline Mode
QR codes work **without internet**. The QR contains a URL to the member's profile. If the scanner is offline, the URL is saved and the profile loads when they're back online.

### Privacy Controls
When someone scans your QR code, they see your **public profile**. Your email, availability, and contribution graph are only visible if you've set them to public.

---

### Deeper Explanation: Friction Is The Enemy In The Room

QR-based networking removes the two frictions of conference introductions: finding someone's profile and typing their details. A short link or QR to a member's community profile (with a "connect" action and an optional note) turns a hallway chat into a real connection in a few seconds.

Practical details that decide whether it works: profiles must be fast on mobile data, the connect action should be one tap with a note, both sides must record the connection, and it must work offline-ish (fallback: scanning a code that stores a link). Privacy: the QR should reveal only what the member chose to publish.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Slow mobile pages.** Any delay kills the moment; optimise the profile route for mobile.
- **Contact details in the QR.** Sharing a phone number by accident is a real risk; share a profile link.
- **No mutual record.** If only one side gets the connection, the networking is one-sided.
- **Requiring an app.** Web-first with a QR link works for everyone.
- **No offline fallback.** Venue Wi-Fi is unreliable; handle failure gracefully.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **No QR networking** → profile exchange is awkward. Connections are lost.
- **No offline mode** → QR codes don't work at venues with poor WiFi.
- **No privacy controls** → members don't display QR codes because they don't want everything public.
- **No mutual opt-in** → connections are one-sided. The other person doesn't know they've been connected.
