### What Community Q&A Is
A community Q&A is a **persistent, searchable layer for community questions and answers**. It answers: **"Has anyone asked this before? What was the answer?"**

The problem: WhatsApp is a **stream, not a database**. Questions are asked, answered, and then buried under 500 subsequent messages. The same question is asked again next month.

The Q&A layer **extracts value from the stream** and makes it persistent.

### Q&A Data Model
```text
Question:
─────────────────────────────────
id: uuid
title: string ("How do I handle auth in Next.js App Router?")
body: text
asked_by: Member
asked_at: timestamp
topic_tags: string[]
answers: Answer[]
  - body: text
  - answered_by: Member
  - upvotes: number
  - accepted: boolean
status: enum (open, answered, closed)
```

### Integration with WhatsApp
A WhatsApp bot can **forward questions** from the group to the Q&A platform. The question is posted, and answers from the group are collected. The question and best answer are then **posted back to WhatsApp** as a summary.

---

### Deeper Explanation: Q&A Is The Community's Searchable Memory

A Q&A space (forum or discussions) compounds: good answers remain discoverable and reduce repeat questions. The properties that make it work are search (titles that describe the problem in the words a searcher would use), accepted answers, and a culture of answering rather than gatekeeping.

Moderation is a real design requirement: accepted answers, merge duplicates, flag low-quality replies, and give newcomers a safe way to ask. Technical touches that help: syntax-highlighted code blocks, snippet sharing (CodeSandbox/StackBlitz), and tag vocabulary shared with the rest of the platform.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Chat-only support.** Knowledge disappears; the same question is asked weekly.
- **No accepted answers.** Readers cannot tell which answer is correct.
- **Gatekeeping "just search".** It suppresses participation from the people you want to keep.
- **Tag sprawl.** Search degrades; reuse one vocabulary across the platform.
- **No moderation.** Low-quality or hostile replies drive contributors away.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **No Q&A layer** → the same question is asked every month. Answers are lost in the WhatsApp stream.
- **No search** → you can't find past answers. You have to ask again.
- **No accepted answers** → the best answer is buried under 10 replies.
- **No topic tagging** → you can't browse by topic.
