### What a Project Showcase Is
A project showcase is a **curated gallery of what the community has built**. It answers: **"What are people in this community building?"**

React Kolkata already has Project Parbon - a project showcase program where top 3 finalists receive **1 month of mentorship from GSoC contributors and industry experts**. The platform showcase makes **every submitted project** visible, not just the top 3.

### The Project-to-Job Pipeline
```text
Project showcase → Hiring manager sees project → Contacts builder → Job opportunity
```

The showcase is not just a gallery. It is a **portfolio layer** that connects builders to opportunities.

### Deeper Explanation: A Showcase Is A Gallery With Metadata

A project showcase only works if entries are structured enough to browse: what it is, who built it, the stack, the status (prototype/shipped), and links. That metadata enables the two valuable flows â€” "show me React projects built by people near me" and "who built this and can I help?" â€” neither of which is possible in a flat list of links.

Entry quality comes from a guided submission form (problem, demo, stack, repo, lessons) rather than a free-text description; the "lessons learned" field is what turns a link into knowledge the community can use.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Links without context.** Nobody clicks; a paragraph of context and a screenshot changes that.
- **No status field.** Prototypes and shipped products get mixed and both suffer.
- **No ownership/attribution.** Contributors want credit; anonymous entries lose trust.
- **No categories or tags.** Browsing becomes impossible past a few dozen projects.
- **No curation or moderation.** Dead links and self-promotion spam degrade the gallery.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **No showcase** → projects are built and forgotten. No one sees them after the demo.
- **Only showcasing winners** → 90% of projects are invisible. The showcase should include every submitted project.
- **No tech stack metadata** → hiring managers can't filter by skills.
- **No builder profile link** → you can't connect a project to its builder.
