## 18.0 The Form Problem

Forms are the most complex part of frontend engineering. They look simple but quickly become unmanageable as requirements grow. The core challenges are:

- **Re-renders** - controlled inputs re-render the entire form on every keystroke.
- **Validation timing** - when do you show errors? On change? On blur? On submit?
- **Error mapping** - server errors must map back to specific fields.
- **Dynamic fields** - add/remove/reorder arrays of inputs.
- **Conditional fields** - show/hide fields based on other field values.
- **Multi-step wizards** - preserve state across steps, validate per step.
- **Accessibility** - errors must be associated with fields for screen readers.
- **Server integration** - Server Actions, progressive enhancement, native `FormData`.

The 2026 consensus is clear: **don't hand-roll form state**. Use a form library with a schema resolver.
