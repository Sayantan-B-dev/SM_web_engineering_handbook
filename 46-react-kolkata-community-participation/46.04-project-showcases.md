### Project Parbon - React Kolkata's Flagship Showcase
Project Parbon is React Kolkata's **flagship project showcase program**. "Parbon" is a Bengali word that represents **festivals - moments of creativity, joy, and community**. Project Parbon is described as a celebration where technology meets culture, and it is explicitly **not a hackathon**. 

> "This is not a hackathon. There's no rush. Developers will submit their projects, and from them, three finalists will be selected to receive one month of mentorship from elite mentors including GSoC contributors and industry leaders."

### Why Project Parbon Is Not a Hackathon
Hackathons are **time-boxed, competitive, and rushed**. Project Parbon is **showcase-oriented, celebratory, and mentorship-driven**. The goal is not to build something in 48 hours - it is to **showcase what you've already built**, share your journey, and connect with a community that values creativity and craftsmanship.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Only showing polished wins.** The learning is in the constraints and the failures.
- **No technical depth.** A feature tour teaches nothing; show the decision.
- **No way to try it.** A repo, a link, or a QR code makes the demo actionable.
- **Overselling.** Claims that do not survive questions damage credibility.
- **No feedback loop.** Ask one specific question so the room can help.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **Treating Project Parbon like a hackathon** → it's not. There's no rush, no teams, no 48-hour deadline. Submit your best work.
- **Missing the submission window** → the portal opens on 17th March and closes on 5th April. Set a reminder.
- **Not joining the WhatsApp community** → updates, mentorship sessions, and submission support happen there.
