### What Project Parbon Is
Project Parbon is React Kolkata's **flagship project showcase program** - a celebration of builders. "Parbon" is a Bengali word representing **festivals - moments of creativity, joy, and community**. 

> "Project Parbon is our way of celebrating the incredible projects built by developers. This is not a hackathon. There's no rush. Developers will submit their projects, and from them, three finalists will be selected to receive one month of mentorship from elite mentors including GSoC contributors and industry leaders. They will also be specially invited to React Kolkata's first offline event." 

### How to Participate
1. **Register** on the Project Parbon portal (opens 17th March)
2. **Join the WhatsApp community** for updates and support
3. **Submit your project** between 17th March and 5th April
4. **Attend the mentorship session** (special session for doubt-solving and industry best practices) 
5. **Wait for evaluation** (5th-10th April)
6. **Winners announced** on 11th April

### Deeper Explanation: Community Projects Need Owners And Milestones

Community projects (Project Parbon in the React Kolkata ecosystem) can be a mentoring vehicle: a real project with a real scope where newcomers contribute with guidance. What makes them work is the same as in professional teams â€” clear ownership, small tasks, visible progress, and a definition of done.

For participants: pick a task labelled for newcomers, ask questions early, submit small PRs, and write down what you learned. For organisers: keep the backlog groomed with genuinely scoped issues, review quickly (responsiveness is what maintains motivation), and celebrate completed milestones so the value is visible.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **No owner.** Projects stall when nobody is responsible for unblocking.
- **Backlog of vague tasks.** Newcomers cannot start; scope issues before publishing them.
- **Slow reviews.** Contributors disappear while waiting; review within a day or two.
- **No milestone.** Progress without a definition of done loses momentum.
- **Scope creep into a "product".** Keep it a learning project with a bounded goal.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **Missing the submission window** → the portal closes on 5th April. Set a reminder.
- **Not joining the WhatsApp community** → updates, mentorship sessions, and support happen there. 
- **Treating it like a hackathon** → there's no rush. Submit your best existing work, not a rushed weekend project.
