### What Lightning Talks Are
Lightning talks are **short presentations, typically 5-10 minutes**, that fit many speakers into one event. They are the **ideal entry point for first-time speakers** - short enough to prepare carefully, low stakes, and high learning density.

A 5-minute lightning talk is a **sprint**. You have time for exactly one idea. Every sentence must serve that idea.

```text
✅ Good lightning talk topic: "How React Query eliminates loading spinners"
❌ Bad lightning talk topic: "An overview of React Query, useQuery, useMutation,
   query keys, caching, and optimistic updates"
```

### Why Lightning Talks Matter for React Kolkata
React Kolkata's meetups feature a mix of **full-length talks (20-30 min)** and **lightning talks (5-10 min)** . Lightning talks give first-time speakers a stage without the pressure of a 30-minute slot. They also allow the community to hear from more voices in a single evening.

---

### Deeper Explanation: Five Minutes Is A Career Tool

Lightning talks are the lowest-friction way to become known as someone who knows things: the scope is small, the preparation is bounded, and the format forgives imperfection. The structure that works is one idea, three points, one demo, one takeaway â€” 5 to 7 slides, 5 minutes, rehearsed twice.

Good topics are the ones you already solved recently: a bug that took a day, a library comparison, a small architecture decision, a tool that sped up your workflow. The audience wants something they can try tomorrow morning, not a survey.

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Trying to compress a 30-minute talk.** Cut the scope instead; speed-reading lands nothing.
- **No demo or example.** Concrete beats abstract at this length.
- **Slides full of text.** Five slides of bullet points cannot be read in five minutes.
- **No takeaway sentence.** The audience should be able to repeat your one idea.
- **Waiting for a "worthy" topic.** Your recent small win is exactly the right level.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **Trying to cover too much** → one idea per lightning talk. Cut ruthlessly.
- **No timer** → you run over. Practice with a timer.
- **Code slides too small** → the audience can't read them. 18pt minimum, 5-10 lines max.
