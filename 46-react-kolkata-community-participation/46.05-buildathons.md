### What a Buildathon Is
A buildathon is a **time-boxed building event** - similar to a hackathon, but often shorter, more focused, and sometimes individual rather than team-based. React Kolkata's buildathons are **tool-specific** (e.g., Miro) and **solo events** where participants compete on their own personal canvas. 

### The Miro Buildathon (August 2026)
Participants had exactly **1 hour** to build anything they wanted using a blank Miro canvas. The challenge was to **showcase visual thinking skills** - how participants used Miro to solve problems, organize workflows, or map out ideas. No Miro expertise was required. 

### Buildathon vs Hackathon

| Dimension | Buildathon | Hackathon |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Duration** | 1-3 hours | 24-72 hours |
| **Team size** | Often solo | Usually teams |
| **Focus** | One tool, one skill | Full product |
| **Output** | Prototype, sketch, workflow | Working demo |
| **Competition** | Light | Intense |

### Deeper Explanation: Constraints Are The Point

A buildathon (hackathon for product thinking) compresses the whole product loop into hours: define scope, decide, build, demo, learn. The teams that succeed are usually the ones that cut hardest â€” one feature, one user path, mocked data â€” rather than the ones with the biggest idea.

Engineers get three things from participating: practice making decisions under time pressure, exposure to unfamiliar stacks or teammates, and a demoable artifact. The reusable habit is the MVP discipline it forces: what is the smallest thing that demonstrates the idea?

### Additional Pitfalls
- **Scope too large.** Teams run out of time and demo a broken skeleton.
- **No integration plan.** People build in parallel and cannot merge by the deadline.
- **Ignoring the demo moment.** The pitch and the happy path matter as much as the code.
- **Building with tools nobody knows.** Learning plus time pressure is a bad combination.
- **No follow-through.** The best buildathon projects continue with the same team afterwards.

### Real-World Deal-Breakers
- **Assuming buildathons require coding** → the Miro Buildathon required no coding. It was about visual thinking and problem-solving. 
- **No time management** → 60 minutes goes fast. Plan your approach before the timer starts.
